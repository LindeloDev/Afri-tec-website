About Afri-Tec

Where Safety Meets Simplicity

At Afri-Tec, we believe that safety should be simple, reliable and accessible.

We provide smart safety, emergency alert, and tracking solutions designed to help people stay connected and protected when it matters most. Our technology brings together GPS tracking, emergency alerts, communication and connected devices to provide practical solutions for individuals, families, businesses and organisations.

Whether it's protecting a loved one, supporting an elderly parent, or helping businesses monitor valuable assets, Afri-Tec provides technology designed for real-world situations.

Our Approach

We focus on three things:

Safety

Reliable technology that helps people respond quickly during an emergency.

Tracking

Helping families and organisations know where their people or assets are.

Connection

Keeping people connected through smart devices and communication technology.

The Problem We Solve

Every second counts
in an emergency

Emergencies don't wait for help to be within reach. The time between something going wrong and someone knowing about it can make all the difference.

Afri-Tec was built to close that gap connecting people, alerts, and location the moment they're needed most.

1

Instant alert delivery

The moment a button is pressed, a signal is sent no waiting, no delay.

2

Real-time location

GPS tracking means responders know exactly where to go, not just that something is wrong.

3

Two-way communication

Direct voice communication so responders always know the full situation.

Trust & Compliance

Trust & Compliance: Reliability, Proven

Real safety doesn't come from a promise it comes from testing, standards, and consistency you can count on. Afri-Tec is held to that bar at every stage.

Compliance & Testing

Before any device reaches you, it earns its place through rigorous checks.

  • ICASA-certified communication & radio equipment
  • Field-tested network coverage and reliability
  • Battery life verified under real-world conditions
  • Alert response times independently monitored

Our devices are ICASA approved, meeting South Africa's regulatory standards for communication and radio equipment but that's our baseline, not our finish line.

Beyond compliance, we test network reliability, battery performance, and alert response times, so that when someone presses that button, the system responds no delays, no failure points.

We also work alongside organisations focused on personal safety and GBV awareness, because protection technology is most powerful when it's part of a wider support system.
