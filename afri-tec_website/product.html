Getting Started

Set up your safety net in 4 steps

No technical know how needed just your phone and a few minutes.

1

Download the app

Get the Afri-Tec SOS app free from the App Store or Google Play and create your account.

2

Pair your device

Turn on your Alert Panic, Lifeline Watch, or Lifeline Pendant and connect it to the app in a few taps.

3

Add your contacts

Choose the people who should be notified first: family, guardians, or your monitoring team.

4

Send a test alert

Try it once so you know exactly what happens and that your contacts do too before you ever need it for real.
